How to drive

Brake lever on the left. Slide it up to let off, down to stop. Power on the right, six notches.

FWD / REV picks the direction. Stop first to change it.

Points: the button shows the next set of points ahead and where they send you. Tap it, or tap the points on the track, to change them. You can't move points with a train sitting on them.

Coupling: run gently into a wagon. Under 4 mph is a soft coupling. Faster than that breaks things inside.

Weight: every wagon makes the train slower to start and much slower to stop. The marker on the track shows where you'd stop on full brake. Red means you won't make it.

Banks: heavy trains stall on hills and slip on full power. Ease off a notch when the wheels spin, take a run at it, or leave a wagon behind. Sand helps.

Fragile loads hate hard braking and couplings. Loose loads spill on fast curves. Watch the curve limit boards.

Deliver: stop with the whole train past the yellow board in the depot goods road, then press Deliver.

Tap a wagon to read its label or leave it behind. Drag the view to look around.
